Private automotive concierge.

Pascucci Prestige gives San Antonio clients seamless access to a curated fleet of luxury and exotic vehicles, delivered with concierge-level service and private-client polish.

Core standard
Drive beyond ordinary. Exotic without the friction.

Brand Essence

The brand balances performance and prestige: sculptural vehicles, controlled motion, editorial space, concierge service, and quiet confidence.

Position

Luxury access, privately handled.

Not a rental counter. Not a dealership. A controlled, high-touch service for curated vehicle access.

Client Need

Special without complicated.

The experience should feel memorable while the process stays clear, fast, and handled.

Personality

Bold, discreet, precise.

Confident language, refined visuals, and service details that build trust before the drive begins.

Logo System

The complete logo remains the primary brand identifier. Preserve the gold speed mark, silver car silhouette, gold wordmark, and Prestige descriptor as a controlled lockup.

Color

Black and ivory carry the system. Gold is premium emphasis. Red is a controlled performance signal. Titanium and graphite connect the palette to the metallic logo and vehicle language.

Typography

Use serif type for prestige and emotion, sans serif for clarity and service, and condensed utility type for details such as rates, dates, locations, and vehicle specifications.

Visual Direction

Photography should be cinematic, architectural, and premium. Let the vehicle dominate, keep human presence calm and professional, and use San Antonio references with restraint.

Use

Vehicle-led scenes.

  • Low-angle three-quarter vehicle views
  • Hotel, airport, and residential delivery
  • Wet pavement, controlled highlights, detail crops
People

Secondary and composed.

  • Well dressed, calm, and professional
  • Natural concierge handoffs
  • No exaggerated poses or wealth cliches
Avoid

No rental-counter energy.

  • Dealership lots and white-background inventory
  • Nightclub neon and extreme HDR
  • Cash, cigars, champagne, or unsafe driving

Application

Campaigns should focus on access, arrival, and personal service. Copy should stay short and controlled.

Quality Checklist

Every piece should reinforce a private, polished experience for exceptional vehicles.

  • Does the vehicle or concierge service feel like the hero?
  • Is the tone confident without being loud?
  • Is gold used with restraint?
  • Is red used only as a signal or CTA?
  • Is the logo legible with enough clear space?
  • Does the layout feel spacious and controlled?
  • Is the copy specific, short, and service-oriented?
  • Does the work avoid dealership, discount, and influencer luxury cues?
  • Are all important details readable on mobile and in print?
  • Does the piece reinforce exotic access without friction?
